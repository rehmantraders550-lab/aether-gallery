# AETHER / Contemporary Museum Editorial

**Concept chosen:** A — The Modern Museum.

## Purpose
A restrained digital exhibition where artwork remains more prominent than interactive surfaces. Prototype theme: **The Language of Remembrance**.

## Identity
- Primary canvas: `#f4f1ec` warm paper
- Gallery dark: `#24231f` charcoal-brown
- Text: `#25231f` / muted `#77736c`
- Prismatic interaction: `linear-gradient(90deg,#4ed8ff 0%,#ff4fa3 52%,#ff8a2a 100%)`
- Primary button: `#f2f1ee` / `#06070a`, 4 px corners, 2→4 px prism accent
- Type: Newsreader (editorial), Manrope (body), JetBrains Mono (catalogue), Amiri (future Arabic text)

## Composition
1. Split museum entrance: charcoal editorial voice + source-faithful triptych on a paper-light gallery surface.
2. Selected works: three image-led panels. No fabricated works or duplicated images masquerading as a collection.
3. Material close-up: source crop, not a reconstructed relief.
4. Viewing room: recessed surface selectors and switchable **surrounding** walls.
5. Curatorial notes: factual provenance constraints.

## Interaction models
- Aether Prism (primary CTA)
- Selectable compositions (single source of truth)
- Modal viewer (zoom, pan, previous/next, reset)
- Tactile neumorphism limited to environmental controls
- Focus, keyboard and reduced-motion fallbacks

## Editorial rule
Art should command the first glance. The typography acts as curator. Controls disappear in importance when not in use.

## Art provenance
All art shown in the prototype is sourced exclusively from the user's `CALIGRAPHY.webp`. The image's authorship, dimensions and physical production process were not supplied, so none are asserted.
